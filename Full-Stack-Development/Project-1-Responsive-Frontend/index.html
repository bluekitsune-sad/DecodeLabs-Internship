What’s baking

Every morning we open the ovens before sunrise. Here is a taste of what regularly makes it to the counter.

Sourdough Loaf

Naturally leavened with a 24-hour ferment. Crackling crust, open crumb, and just enough tang.

Baked: daily at 7:00

Butter Croissant

Forty layers of crisp, flaky pastry made with French butter. Best enjoyed still warm.

Baked: daily at 6:30

Honey & Rye Sourdough

Our signature loaf: a handful of rye, a drizzle of wildflower honey, and a deeply toasted crust.

Baked: Fridays at 8:00

Plum Galette

A rustic pastry of late-summer plums on a sturdy free-form crust. Seasonal and lightly spiced.

Baked: weekends at 9:00

Cinnamon Bun

Soft, pillowy dough, brown sugar and cardamom, finished with a thin vanilla glaze.

Baked: Wednesdays & Sundays

Seeded Flatbread

Whole-wheat flatbread topped with sesame, flax and fennel seed. Great with dips.

Baked: Thursdays at 8:30

Baked slowly since 2019

Hearth & Crumb started with one oven and a stubborn belief: a good loaf needs time. We still shape everything by hand, still mill our flour locally, and still wave at every regular who walks through the door.

Frequently asked questions

Do you bake gluten-free bread?

We do not yet offer a gluten-free range, but our cardamom buns and seeded flatbreads contain no dairy.

Can I order a cake for a celebration?

Yes. Place a custom order at least two days ahead by using the form below or calling us on opening days.

Do you take online delivery orders?

Not yet. We keep everything for the counter, but you can reserve a loaf by phone and pick it up same-day.

Visit the bakery

We are on the corner of Maple and Pine, a ten minute walk from the town square. Come early on weekends — the croissants go fast.
